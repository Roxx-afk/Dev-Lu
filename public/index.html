    Original Ludo rules

    • Each player has 4 tokens in their yard. Roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square.
    • Tokens travel clockwise once around the board, then up your coloured home column into the centre.
    • You need the exact number to reach home.
    • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and your turn is forfeited.
    • Land on a single opponent token to capture it — it goes back to its yard and you roll again.
    • Getting a token home also earns another roll.
    • Safe squares (★ stars and coloured start squares) can’t be captured on.
    • Blockade rule (optional, host’s choice): two tokens of one colour on a square form a block that opponents can’t pass or land on. It comes from the traditional Parcheesi-style rules, but many popular Ludo rule sets leave it out, so it’s off by default.
    • The dice are fair for everyone. Every roll comes from the server’s secure random generator, bots use the same dice, and the first player is chosen at random.
    • If you can’t move, your turn passes.

    Modes

    • Classic — 2–4 players, play until every place is decided.
    • Team — Team A (Red & Yellow) vs Team B (Green & Blue). Pick your team in the lobby with “Join Team A/B”. No capturing your partner. Once all your tokens are home, you keep rolling on your turn and move your partner’s tokens.
    • Quick — one token starts on the board, first to get 2 tokens home wins, 15s turns.
